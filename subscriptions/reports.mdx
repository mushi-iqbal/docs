**Reports → Subscriptions** covers subscriptions started in your dates. Active subscriptions and MRR are counted as they stood on the last day of the period.

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| New Subscription Value | The recurring price of the subscriptions started, added up |
| Active Subscriptions | Active at the end of the period |
| Monthly Recurring Revenue | MRR at the end of the period |
| New Subscriptions | Started in the period |

Each card compares with your comparison period. **New Subscriptions Over Time** charts signups by day, week or month; dashed lines show the compared period.

**Top-Selling** and **Slow-Moving Subscription Products** list the plans with the most and fewest new subscriptions, with units and revenue.
