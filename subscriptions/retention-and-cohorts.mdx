## Subscription retention

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/subscription-retention.png" alt="Subscriptions Retention: subscription retention, MRR retention, subscription churn and MRR churn, with charts over time" />
</Frame>

**Reports → Subscriptions Retention** looks at each **complete month**: of the subscriptions active on the 1st, how many were still active at the month's end.

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Subscription Retention | Share of subscriptions active on the 1st still active at the end |
| MRR Retention | The same for their MRR |
| Subscription Churn, MRR Churn | What was lost: 100% minus retention |

Cards average the months in your range. Charts show retention and churn month by month, the **Monthly Breakdown** table gives each month's figures, and **Expected Lifetime** turns your average churn into how long a subscription lasts and what it's worth over that time.

<Note>Only complete months count. Pick a range that includes at least one full month.</Note>

## Subscription cohorts

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/subscription-cohorts.png" alt="Subscription Cohort Retention: subscriptions grouped by start month and how many stayed active in each later month" />
</Frame>

**Cohorts → Subscriptions Cohorts** groups subscriptions by the month they started and shows how many were still active in each month after. Switch between counts (#) and percentages (%), and group by month, week or year. Read it like a [customer cohort](/advanced/cohort-analysis/what-is-cohort-analysis): rows are starting months, columns are months since.

Healthy subscription businesses often keep 80% or more at month 3; a sharp drop in the first month usually points to onboarding or a first delivery that disappoints.
