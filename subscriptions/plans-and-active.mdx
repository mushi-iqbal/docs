## Subscription plans

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/subscription-plans.png" alt="Subscription Plans: active subscriptions and MRR per product for each month, with a monthly MRR chart" />
</Frame>

**Reports → Subscription Plans** shows, for each subscription product, how many subscriptions were active and their MRR at the end of every complete month. The **All Products** row totals them, and the chart shows your monthly MRR and active subscriptions together.

Use it to see which plans are growing, which have stalled, and how a price change or new plan shifted your MRR.

## Active subscriptions

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/active-subscriptions.png" alt="Active Subscriptions: active subscriptions, total MRR, total ARR and average MRR, grouped by billing country" />
</Frame>

**Reports → Active Subscriptions** describes right now:

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Active Subscriptions | Active right now |
| Total MRR / ARR | Their monthly recurring revenue, and × 12 |
| Average MRR | MRR per active subscription |

Group them by **billing country**, **shipping country** or **payment method**, and **Export CSV**.

<Note>
  **MRR** converts every billing schedule to a month: a $199 yearly plan adds $16.58, a $49 every-3-months plan adds $16.33. On hold and pending subscriptions don't count as active.
</Note>
