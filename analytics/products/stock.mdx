**Products → Stock** shows each product's stock status and quantity next to what it sold in your dates.

## The cards

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Total Products | All your products |
| Out of Stock | Products out of stock or at zero |
| Untracked Stock | Products whose stock isn't managed in WooCommerce |
| Est. Inventory Value | Current price × stock quantity, for products with managed stock |

Click a card to filter the list. Each product shows **Low Stock**, **In Stock**, **Out of Stock** or **Untracked**, its sales and units in your dates, and its stock quantity. Filter by name, category, price and stock quantity.

## Low stock level and alerts

In **Settings → Store Settings → Notifications**:

- **Low stock level**: products at or below this many units count as low stock, here and on the dashboard's Low / Out of Stock widget.
- **Stock Alert Emails**: get an email when products go out of stock or reach your low-stock level. SaleTides checks every hour and only emails about products that newly changed. Send it to your account email or another address.

Stock levels update whenever WooCommerce sends a product change, and with every product import.
