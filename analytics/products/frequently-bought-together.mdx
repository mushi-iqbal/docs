---
title: "Frequently bought together"
description: "On each product's page: the products most often in the same orders."
---

The **Frequently Bought With** section on a product's page lists the other products that appear most often in orders containing it, with how many orders they share.

## Reading it

A product near the top is often added to the same basket. That's a cue for:

- **Bundles** at a small discount.
- **"Customers also bought"** suggestions on the product page.
- **Merchandising**: placing them together in emails and collections.

**Include free products** adds products that were free in those orders (gifts, samples); they're left out by default because they're rarely chosen by the customer. **View Orders** opens the orders behind a pairing.

## Things to know

- It follows the product page's date range; a longer range gives steadier results.
- For a variable product it looks at all its variations; on a variation's page, at that variation only.
- At category level, a category page's **Bought with** shows which other categories share its orders. See [Categories](/analytics/products/categories).
