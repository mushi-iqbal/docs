Open **Products** and choose the **Variations** tab to list every variation, such as **Navy / L** of **Classic Hoodie**, on its own row. The tab only appears if your store sells variable products.

Each row shows the variation and its parent **Product**, with the variation's **Net Sold** and **Net Sales** in your dates. Click a variation to open its page, or its product name to open the parent product.

It works like the Products tab:

- **Cards**: Variation Sales, Items Sold, Variations Sold and Average Item Price.
- **Sort** by any column, including the parent product.
- **Type**: **One-off** or **Subscription** keeps the variations of those products.
- **Smart Filter**: SKU, price, stock, status, first and last order dates and more. **Category** matches the parent product's categories, since WooCommerce keeps categories on the product, not on each variation.
- **Saved filters** are kept per tab: a filter saved on Variations loads there, and **Saved Filters** totals each one's variations, units sold and revenue.
- **Export** sends a CSV with the variation, its product, SKU, price, stock status, net sold and net sales.
- **Customize Columns** is shared with the Products tab.

<Tip>To compare the sizes or colours of one product, open that product's page instead: its **Variations** section ranks its variations by units sold, and the **Attributes** panel lists the options each one has.</Tip>
