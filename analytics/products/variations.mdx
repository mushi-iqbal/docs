**Products → Variations** lists every variation, such as **Classic Hoodie — Navy / L**, with its SKU, parent product, stock status and price.

- **Search** by name, SKU, price or stock status.
- **Smart Filter** and **saved filters** work as on other lists. **Saved Filters** compares saved variation groups side by side: products, units sold and revenue.
- **Export** sends a CSV.

On a variable product's page, the **Variations** section ranks its variations by units sold, so you can see at a glance which sizes or colours sell, and the **Attributes** panel lists the options each variation has.
