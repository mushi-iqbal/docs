**Products → Top Selling Products** ranks products by what they sold in your dates (default: the last 30 days). Variations count toward their product.

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Products Sold | Different products with at least one sale |
| Units Sold | Across all products |
| Revenue | Line totals after discounts |
| Top 10 Share | The share of revenue from your 10 best sellers |

A high **Top 10 Share** means your sales depend on a few products: worth knowing before one of them runs out of stock or a supplier changes prices.

The list shows each product's units, revenue and share of revenue. The top three get medals while the list is ranked best-first. Products since deleted from your store keep the name their orders recorded.
