**Products** lists your products with their **Net Sold** (units) and **Net Sales** (line totals after discounts) in your dates, from orders in your sales statuses. Variations count toward their product; see [Variations](/analytics/products/variations) for each one.

## The cards

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Product Sales | Line totals after discounts, excluding tax and shipping |
| Items Sold | Units sold |
| Products Sold | Products with at least one sale |
| Average Item Price | Product sales per unit |

The cards follow your search, filters and tab.

## One-off and subscription products

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-type-tabs.png" alt="The All products, One-off and Subscriptions tabs" />
</Frame>

If you sell with WooCommerce Subscriptions, three tabs appear above the cards: **All products**, **One-off** and **Subscriptions**. The list, the cards and the export follow the tab you choose, and it stays selected when you change the dates.

Subscription products also carry a **Subscription** badge next to their name, and the **Type** column shows every product's type in plain words: Simple, Variable, Subscription, Variable subscription, Grouped, External.

## Columns and filters

The default columns are product, type, date created, net sold and net sales. **Customize Columns** adds SKU, prices, stock, status, featured, on sale, rating, categories and more.

The Smart Filter covers 30+ fields, including category, type, price, stock status, first and last order dates and sales. **Type** and **Category** offer a list to choose from.

## A product's page

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-detail.png" alt="A product's page: price, net orders, net sold and net revenue, with its variations" />
</Frame>

Click a product to see, for your dates:

- **Totals**: price, net orders, net sold and net revenue.
- **Variations**: each variation's units sold, and for subscription products its active subscriptions and MRR.
- **Sales statistics**: a chart by day, week or month, with average order value, units per order and the best period.
- **Spending by hour and by day of the week.**
- **Sales by customer**, and **Sales analytics** grouped by billing or shipping location, order status or payment method.
- **Subscriptions** that include it (subscription products only).
- **[Frequently bought with](/analytics/products/frequently-bought-together)**: what else is in its orders.
- **Core details**: type, stock, prices, rating, tax, shipping and categories. Each category links to its [category page](/analytics/products/categories).
