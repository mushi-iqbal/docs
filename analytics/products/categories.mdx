**Products → Categories** shows what each of your WooCommerce product categories sold in orders placed in your dates (in your sales statuses).

## The cards

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Product Sales | All product sales in your dates, every category |
| Categories with Sales | Categories that sold anything, out of all your categories |
| Top Category | The top-level category with the most sales, and its share |
| Uncategorised Sales | Sales of products in no category |

The chart below follows your six biggest top-level categories over time.

## The categories table

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/categories-table.png" alt="The categories table with Clothing expanded into Hoodies and T-Shirts, and Home into Decor and Drinkware" />
</Frame>

For each category: **net sales** (line totals after discounts), its **share** of all product sales, **items sold**, **orders** and **customers**, **sales per order**, and the change against your comparison period ("New" when it sold nothing before).

- **Parent categories include their sub-categories.** Clothing's row covers Hoodies and T-Shirts; click the arrow to see them, or **Expand all**. A product in both a parent and its sub-category counts once in the parent.
- **A product in two categories counts in both.** A hoodie in "Hoodies" and "Sale" adds to both rows, so the rows can add up to more than your product sales. Metorik and WooCommerce Analytics count the same way.
- **Variations count toward their product**, and so toward its categories.

Search, sort by any column, and **Export CSV**. The box icon next to a name opens the Products list filtered to that category.

## A category's page

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/category-page.png" alt="The Clothing category page: net sales, items, orders and customers, its sales over time, categories bought with it, sub-categories, products and top customers" />
</Frame>

Click a category to see:

- **Net sales, items, orders and customers**, each against your comparison period, with sales per order and how many customers were new.
- **Sales over time.**
- **Bought with**: the other categories most often in the same orders, as a share of this category's orders.
- **Sub-categories**, **products** ranked by sales (with their share of the category) and **top customers** by spend.

## Categories elsewhere

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| Orders list | A **Categories** column, and the **Product Category** Smart Filter |
| Customers list | The **Bought from Category** Smart Filter |
| Products list | A **Categories** column and the Category filter |
| Revenue → Revenue Grouping | Group by **Product Category** |
| Dashboard | The **Sales by Category** widget |
