**Reports → Customer Groups** takes the customers who placed their first order in your dates and groups them, so you can compare groups on what they went on to spend.

## Grouping options

| Tab | Groups customers by |
|---|---|
| By First Ordered Product | The product in their first order |
| By Role | Their WordPress role (customer, subscriber, wholesale and so on; guests are "guest") |
| By Billing Country | Their billing country |
| By Shipping Country | Their shipping country |

## Reading the table

Each group shows its customers, their lifetime spend (total and per customer), their lifetime orders and how many came back, compared with your comparison period. Sort by any column; **View customers in this report** opens the customer list.

## Use cases

- **Which first product makes loyal customers?** Sort **By First Ordered Product** by average lifetime value. Products whose buyers keep spending are worth featuring in ads and welcome offers.
- **Which countries are worth more?** Compare average lifetime value by billing country before deciding where to spend on shipping offers.
- **Wholesale against retail.** **By Role** separates customer types that behave very differently.
