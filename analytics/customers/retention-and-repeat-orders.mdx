**Reports → Customer Retention** follows the customers who placed their **first** order in your dates (default: the last 90 days) and shows whether they came back, at any time since.

## The cards

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Joined | Customers whose first order was in your dates |
| Returning | Of those, the ones who ordered again at any time since |
| Retention Rate | Returning divided by joined |
| Orders per Customer | Their lifetime orders, per customer |

## Repeat orders

- **New customers by lifetime orders**: how many placed 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5+ orders, with their average lifetime value.
- **One-time and returning customers**: lists of each, linked to their profiles.
- **Time between repeat orders**: how long returning customers take to order again. Recent groups show "Not enough time yet" until there's enough history.

## Improving retention

- The first weeks after a first order matter most: a thank-you, a how-to or a reorder reminder at the right moment.
- Compare **Time between repeat orders** with your product's natural reorder cycle; a reminder just before it helps.
- Pick a range a few months back: recent customers haven't had time to return yet.

For month-by-month retention, see the [Returning Customers cohort](/advanced/cohort-analysis/retention-cohorts).
