**Customers** lists the customers who **joined** in your dates (placed their first order in them), with what they've done over their whole lifetime since. Pick "All Time" to see everyone.

<Note>
  **Guest checkouts are grouped by email.** WooCommerce gives every guest order the same customer number, so SaleTides groups a guest's orders into one profile per email address. If that guest later registers with the same email, their guest orders join their account.
</Note>

## The cards

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Customers | Customers who joined in your dates and match your search and filters |
| Total Spent | Their lifetime spend in your sales statuses, less refunds |
| Average LTV | Total spent per customer |
| Total Orders, Orders per Customer | Their lifetime orders, in total and per customer |
| Average Order Value | Total spent per order |
| Items Purchased, Items per Order | Units in their orders |
| Active Subscriptions | Active subscriptions these customers hold today |

Filter customers with the Smart Filter (spend, order count, country, role, first and last order, products bought, categories bought from and more), choose columns, save filters and export. **Bought from Category** includes the category's sub-categories, so "Clothing" finds everyone who bought a hoodie or a T-shirt.

## A customer's profile

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/customer-profile.png" alt="A customer's profile: total orders, total spent, average order value, last order, unique products, timeline and products purchased" />
</Frame>

Click a customer to see:

- **Totals**: orders, total spent, average order value, last order and unique products.
- **Customer timeline**: their orders and subscription events in order.
- **Products purchased**: everything they've bought.
- **Billing and shipping addresses.**
- **Subscription history** (with WooCommerce Subscriptions).
- **Customer orders**: every order, searchable, with its relationship to a subscription.
