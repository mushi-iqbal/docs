**Reports → Customer Sources** credits each customer to the source of their **first** order, then follows what they spent since. It answers "which channels bring customers who keep buying?", which order-level reports can't.

| Tab | Groups customers by their first order's |
|---|---|
| Referrals | Referring site (Google, Facebook, direct…) |
| Landing Pages | First page visited |
| UTM | UTM source, medium and campaign |

Each row shows customers, their orders, revenue and average order value, and how many came back.

Sources come from WooCommerce Order Attribution (WooCommerce 8.5+); customers whose first order has no source are grouped as direct or unknown. For order-level sources, see [Order sources and devices](/advanced/traffic-sources).
