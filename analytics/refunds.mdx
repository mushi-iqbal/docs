## Refunds overview

The **Refunds** section gives you a complete view of all refunds issued on your store. Navigate to **Refunds** in the sidebar.

## Refund metrics

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total Refunds** | Number of refund records in the selected period |
| **Refunded Amount** | Total value refunded |
| **Refund Rate** | Refunded amount as a % of gross revenue |
| **Avg Refund Amount** | Average value per refund |

## Refunds list

The refunds table shows all refunds with:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Order # | Linked WooCommerce order |
| Date | Date refund was issued |
| Customer | Customer name and email |
| Reason | Refund reason (if recorded in WooCommerce) |
| Amount | Refund amount |
| Status | Refund status |

Click any row to view the linked order details.

## Filtering refunds

- **Date range** — refunds issued in a specific period
- **Amount range** — refunds above or below a threshold
- **Customer** — search by customer name or email
- **Payment method** — see refunds by payment gateway

## Refund trends

The **Trends** chart shows your refund count and amount over time. Use this to:
- Spot spikes in refund activity (may indicate a product issue or fulfillment problem)
- Track whether your refund rate is improving or worsening
- Compare refund behavior across time periods

## Refunds by payment method

A breakdown of refunds grouped by the payment method used on the original order. Useful for understanding if certain gateways have higher refund rates.

## Monthly analytics

The monthly refund report shows month-by-month:
- Total refunds issued
- Total amount refunded
- Refund rate as % of gross revenue

## Why your refund rate matters

A high or rising refund rate can indicate:
- Product quality issues
- Misleading product descriptions
- Fulfillment or shipping problems
- Customer satisfaction issues

SaleTides tracks trends so you can catch problems early.

## Exporting refund data

Click **Export** to download the refunds list as CSV or Excel.
