---

SaleTides dates every refund by when it was **issued**, not when the order was placed. A refund in March for a January order appears in March's refunds and comes off March's net sales; January stays as reported.

## Refunds report

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/refunds-report.png" alt="Refunds report: refunds, refunded amount, average refund and refunds over time" />
</Frame>

**Reports → Refunds** shows the number of refunds, the amount paid back and the average refund, compared with your comparison period, and a chart of refunds over time. Below it:

- **By Reason**: refunds grouped by the reason entered in WooCommerce.
- **By Location**: refunds by billing or shipping country, city, state or ZIP, or by shipping company.

## Refunds list

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/refunds-list.png" alt="The refunds list: order, customer, email, amount, reason, date refunded and gateway" />
</Frame>

**Refunds** in the menu (below Orders) lists every refund issued in your dates: the order, customer, amount, reason, date and whether it went back through the payment gateway. The cards show refunds, refunded amount (including any tax and shipping refunded), average refund and **Gateway Refunds**: those paid back to the original payment. The rest were refunded manually, for example in cash or store credit.

Filter refunds with the Smart Filter (amount, reason, date, order status, product, country, payment and 20+ more fields), save filters and export to CSV.

## Why your refund rate matters

The dashboard's **Refund Rate** widget shows the share of orders placed in the period that were refunded, fully or partly. A rising rate points to product, sizing or delivery problems; **By Reason** usually tells you which.
