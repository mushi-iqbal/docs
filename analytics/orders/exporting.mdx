## How to export

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the list">Pick your dates, search and Smart Filters. The export follows them.</Step>
  <Step title="Click Export">The button shows how many rows you'll get, for example **Export 245 Orders**.</Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">A download link arrives within a few minutes. It works for 48 hours.</Step>
</Steps>

## What you can export

| From | The CSV has |
|---|---|
| Orders | One row per order: order number, customer, total, date, status and origin |
| Customers | One row per customer with their totals |
| Products | One row per product with its sales; the Products tab you're on (All, One-off, Subscriptions) applies |
| Subscriptions, Coupons, Refunds | One row per record |
| Saved filters | Each saved filter's totals |
| Dashboard lists | Recent orders, top products, top customers and others: use **Export** on the widget |
| Categories | Instantly from **Export CSV** on the Categories report |

<Note>
  The Orders CSV has the six columns above, whatever columns you've chosen on screen.
</Note>

## Large exports

Big exports are built in the background in chunks, so even hundreds of thousands of rows are fine; they may take a little longer to arrive. If no email comes within 15 minutes, check your spam folder, then [contact support](/troubleshooting/contacting-support).
