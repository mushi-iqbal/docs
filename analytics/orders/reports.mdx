**Reports → Orders** covers your **sales orders**: orders in your sales statuses, plus orders refunded since (they were sales when placed). That's the difference from the Orders list, which shows every order in any status.

## The cards

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Net Sales | Gross sales less discounts, tax, shipping and refunds issued in the period |
| Gross Sales | Order totals before coupons and store credit |
| Orders | Sales orders placed |
| Average Order Net / Gross | Net or gross sales per order |
| Discounts | Coupons and store credit |
| Taxes, Shipping | Charged on these orders |
| Refunds | Refunds issued in the period, whenever the order was placed |
| Items Sold, Items per Order | Units in these orders |

## Fulfilment

How quickly orders go from placed to **Completed**: the average time, the share completed within 24 hours and within 7 days, and how many completed orders the figures are based on. Only orders with a completion date count.

## Breakdowns

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| Top Products | Products by sales in these orders, filterable by category |
| Payment Grouping | Sales by payment method |
| Customer Grouping | Sales by billing or shipping location |
| Order Status | Orders by status |

A **New vs. Returning Customers** table splits the period's orders and sales between first-time and repeat customers. **View Orders** opens the Orders list with the same dates.
