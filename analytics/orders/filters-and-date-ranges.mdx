## Date ranges

Pick a preset or a custom range at the top of the page. On the Orders list the range applies to the date each order was placed, in your store's timezone. See [Finding your way around](/getting-started/navigating-the-dashboard#dates-and-comparisons) for comparisons.

## Smart Filters

<Frame caption="Orders that include a product from the Subscriptions category">
  <img src="/images/smart-filter.png" alt="A Smart Filter row: Product Category, Includes, Subscriptions" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type what to filter by">
    In the Smart Filter box, type a field (for example "category", "country" or "coupon") and pick it, or click a **Quick add** chip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a condition and value">
    Conditions depend on the field: equals, contains, starts with, greater than, between, includes, is empty and so on. Fields with a fixed set of values (status, country, category, payment method) offer a list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search & Filter">
    Add more rows to narrow further; every row must match. The cards and the count follow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Some useful filters

| To see | Filter |
|---|---|
| Failed payments this month | Status is Failed |
| Orders from one category | Product Category includes Coffee (sub-categories are included) |
| Big orders | Order Total greater than 200 |
| A campaign's orders | UTM Campaign equals black_friday |
| Coupon orders | Coupon Code is not empty |
| Orders from returning customers | Customer Type is Returning Customer |
| Orders with one product | Order Products includes Classic Hoodie |

## Saved filters

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/saved-filters.png" alt="Saved Filters: each saved filter with its orders, gross sales, items, average order value and items per order" />
</Frame>

Click **Save Filter** to keep a combination. **Saved Filters** lists them with their totals side by side, and **Load a Saved Filter** applies one to the list. See [Saved filters](/advanced/segments/overview).

## Clearing filters

**Clear Filters** removes every row and shows all orders in your dates again.
