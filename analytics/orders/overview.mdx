**Orders** lists every order placed in the dates you pick, whatever its status. The cards above it total the orders in the list and follow your search and filters, so filtering to "Status is Failed" shows what failed payments cost you.

<Note>
  The Orders cards cover every order in the list, in any status. For sales only (your sales statuses), see [Revenue](/analytics/revenue) or the [Orders report](/analytics/orders/reports).
</Note>

## The list

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/orders-list.png" alt="The orders list with ID, customer, total, date, subscription relationship, status, origin and categories columns" />
</Frame>

The default columns are order number, customer, total (with payment method), date and status. **Customize Columns** adds any of 25+ more: email, phone, billing and shipping country, payment method, discount, shipping, tax, paid and completed dates, device, source, UTM fields, categories and, for subscription stores, the order's relationship to a subscription (sign-up, renewal, resubscribe or switch).

Click a column header to sort. Click a row to open the order.

## An order's page

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/order-detail.png" alt="An order's page: status, totals, payment method, customer and order items" />
</Frame>

Each order shows its status, totals and payment, the customer (linked to their profile), billing and shipping addresses, its items, order notes, and **Source & Session Data**: where the customer came from (referral, landing page, UTM tags, device). Subscription orders link to their subscription. **Edit Order** opens the order in WooCommerce, and the arrows step through orders.
