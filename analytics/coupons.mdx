**Coupons** lists your WooCommerce coupons with what each did in orders placed in your dates (in your sales statuses): orders, discount given and sales, next to its settings (amount, type, lifetime uses, expiry).

## The cards

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Coupons Used | Different coupon codes used on at least one order |
| Orders with Coupons | Each order counted once, however many coupons it used |
| Discount Given | What these coupons took off |
| Sales | Order totals (after discounts) of the orders that used them |

Tick **Only coupons used in these dates** to hide coupons nobody used. Filter coupons by code, amount, status, discount type, dates, usage and more with the Smart Filter, save filters, and export to CSV.

## A coupon's page

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/coupon-detail.png" alt="A coupon's page: its settings, usage statistics and a chart of uses over time" />
</Frame>

Click a coupon to see:

- **Coupon usage statistics**: first and last use, discount given, sales generated and orders, with a chart over time.
- **Products most frequently purchased with the coupon.**
- **Orders using this coupon**, each linked to the order.
- **Coupon conditions**: product and category restrictions, minimum and maximum spend, usage limits, email restrictions and expiry.
- **Used by**: the customers who used it.

## Ideas

- Compare a launch coupon's sales with the discount it cost.
- Find coupons that are used but restricted to the wrong products.
- Save "Coupon Code is not empty" as a filter on the Orders list to follow discounted orders over time.
