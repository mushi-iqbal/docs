## Requirements

Subscription reports appear when your store runs **WooCommerce Subscriptions** and its subscriptions have been imported. See [Subscriptions not showing](/troubleshooting/subscriptions-not-showing) if they're missing.

## The subscriptions list

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/subscriptions-overview.png" alt="Subscriptions: subscriptions, active now, MRR, ARR, customers, signup revenue, renewals and renewal revenue" />
</Frame>

**Subscriptions** lists the subscriptions started in your dates (default: All Time).

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Subscriptions | Started in your dates |
| Active Now | Of those, the ones that haven't ended today |
| MRR / ARR | What the active ones bill per month (yearly and other schedules converted) and × 12 |
| Customers | Different customers with these subscriptions |
| Signup Revenue | What was paid for the orders that started them |
| Renewals, Renewal Revenue | Their paid renewal orders and what they brought; failed renewals aren't counted |

The cards follow your search and filters. Signup revenue, renewals and customers compare with your comparison period; Active Now, MRR and ARR describe today.

## Columns and filters

The default columns are subscription, customer, total, items (with the product and billing schedule), next payment, end date and status. **Customize Columns** adds start date, billing schedule, MRR, renewals, renewal revenue, last payment, cancelled date, trial end, email, country and payment method. Every column sorts.

Filter by status, product, total, billing period, dates, renewals, active now and more; save filters and export.

## A subscription's page

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/subscription-detail.png" alt="A subscription's page: recurring amount, billing schedule, revenue generated, payment method, schedule dates and customer" />
</Frame>

Each subscription shows its recurring amount and schedule, the revenue it has generated, payment method, start, next payment and end dates, the customer, its items, and its related orders: the signup and every renewal, resubscription and switch.
