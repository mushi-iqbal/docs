**Reports → Revenue** shows what your sales orders brought in. It counts orders in your sales statuses, plus orders refunded since, in the period they were placed.

## The cards

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| Net Revenue | Gross sales minus discounts, tax, shipping, and refunds issued in the period |
| Gross Sales | Order totals before coupons and store credit |
| Orders | Sales orders placed |
| Average Order Net | Net revenue per order |
| Discounts | Coupons and store credit |
| Taxes, Shipping | Charged on these orders |
| Refunds | Issued in the period, whenever the order was placed. **View refunded orders** opens them |
| New / Returning Customer Revenue | Net revenue from first-time and from repeat customers |

Refunds come off net revenue in the period they're issued, the way Metorik and WooCommerce Analytics count them: a refund never changes a month you've already reported.

## Revenue over time

The chart shows gross sales, net revenue and orders by day, week or month; drag across it to zoom in. The **Revenue Breakdown** tab has the same figures as a table, one row per period, with a CSV export.

## Revenue Grouping

<Frame caption="Revenue grouped by product category">
  <img src="/images/revenue-grouping.png" alt="Revenue Grouping by Product Category: orders, net revenue, average net revenue, gross sales and average gross sales for each category" />

Group the period's revenue by:

| Group | Options |
|---|---|
| Billing | Country, state, city, ZIP |
| Shipping | Country, state, city, ZIP, company |
| Order | Status, payment method, currency, created via, order type |
| Customer | Customer role |
| UTM / Attribution | Campaign, source, medium, content, term, device type |
| Product | Product category |

Each row shows orders, net revenue, average net revenue, gross sales and average gross sales. Click an order count to see those orders.

<Note>
  An order's products can belong to several categories, so **Product Category** works from order lines instead: a category's revenue is its products' line totals (after discounts for net, before for gross), and its orders are those with something from the category. Sub-categories are listed under their parent ("Clothing › Hoodies"), and the parent includes them. Click a category to open its [category page](/analytics/products/categories).
</Note>
