---
title: "Numbers don't match?"
description: "Why SaleTides' figures can differ from WooCommerce, Metorik or your payment provider, and how to check."
---

Most differences come from counting different things. Check these first.

## Common causes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Different order statuses">
    SaleTides' sales figures count the statuses chosen in **Settings → Store Settings → Sales Reporting** (Completed and Processing by default), plus orders refunded since. WooCommerce Analytics and Metorik have their own settings; if one includes On hold or Failed orders and the other doesn't, totals differ. The **Orders** list shows every status; its cards are not sales figures.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Refunds counted on a different date">
    SaleTides takes a refund off net sales on the date it was **issued**. A report that removes it from the original order's date will show different monthly figures, though totals over a long period agree.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Gross and net mean different things">
    Gross sales are order totals **before** coupons and store credit, including tax and shipping. Net sales take off discounts, tax, shipping and refunds. The ⓘ on every card says exactly what it counts; the [glossary](/metrics/glossary) has them all.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Timezone">
    SaleTides uses your store's timezone (Settings → Store Settings). An order at 11:30pm can fall on a different day in a tool set to UTC.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Guests counted as one customer per email">
    SaleTides groups a guest's orders by email, so customer counts can be lower than tools that count every guest order as a new customer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A product in several categories">
    On the Categories report a product in two categories counts in both, so categories can add up to more than your product sales.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Free trial import limit">
    During the trial, only your newest 50,000 orders are imported, so all-time figures for very large stores are partial until you subscribe.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payment provider totals">
    Stripe or PayPal payouts differ from sales: they're net of fees, grouped by payout date, and include only their own payments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Checklist before contacting support

- Same date range and timezone in both tools.
- Same order statuses counted.
- Gross against gross, or net against net.
- One order you can see in WooCommerce but not in SaleTides, if any.

## Still different?

Email [support@saletides.com](mailto:support@saletides.com) with the report, the dates and the two figures, and an order number that differs if you have one. We'll trace it.
