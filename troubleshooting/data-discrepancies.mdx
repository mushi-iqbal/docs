## Why numbers might look different

SaleTides and your WooCommerce admin may show different numbers for the same metric. This is almost always explained by one of the following:

## 1. Date range and timezone

SaleTides displays dates in your configured timezone. WooCommerce admin may use UTC or a different timezone. An order placed at 11pm UTC might appear on a different calendar day in SaleTides if your timezone is UTC+2.

**Fix**: Verify your [timezone setting](/settings/localization) in SaleTides matches the timezone used in your WooCommerce reports.

## 2. Order status filters

SaleTides defaults to showing **Completed** and **Processing** orders in most revenue reports. WooCommerce admin "Total Sales" reports may include all order statuses, including Pending and On Hold.

**Fix**: Adjust the order status filter in SaleTides to match what WooCommerce is reporting. Or set it to "All Statuses" for a direct comparison.

## 3. Refund handling

WooCommerce reports may show gross revenue before refunds, net revenue after refunds, or a mix. SaleTides shows both Gross Revenue and Net Revenue — make sure you're comparing the same metric.

**Fix**: Compare SaleTides **Net Revenue** with WooCommerce's net sales figure.

## 4. Import lag

If data was recently added or updated in WooCommerce, it may not yet be reflected in SaleTides (there's a brief delay for webhook processing). For very recent orders (last few minutes), check again in a moment.

## 5. Partial import

If the initial import hasn't completed, or if a manual import was run for a specific date range, older orders may not be in SaleTides yet.

**Fix**: Check the import status from your dashboard. Run a full manual import if needed.

## 6. Guest vs. registered customers

WooCommerce may count guest checkouts differently than registered customers. SaleTides includes both in customer and order counts.

## 7. Tax and shipping inclusion

Depending on your WooCommerce configuration, "total revenue" may or may not include tax and shipping. SaleTides uses the **order total** as recorded in WooCommerce (including tax and shipping).

**Fix**: Verify whether the WooCommerce metric you're comparing to includes or excludes tax/shipping.

## Still seeing a discrepancy?

If after checking all of the above you still see a significant unexplained difference:

1. Note the specific metric, date range, and the values in both SaleTides and WooCommerce
2. Contact support at support@saletides.com with this information

Our team can investigate at the data level to identify the source of the discrepancy.
