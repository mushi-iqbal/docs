## Email

Write to [support@saletides.com](mailto:support@saletides.com). We reply within one business day, and Pro and Scale customers get priority.

## Include

- Your SaleTides address (for example `mystore.saletides.com`).
- The page and the dates you were looking at.
- What you expected and what you saw; a screenshot helps.
- An order number if it's about specific orders.

## From inside the app

- **Report** on the import page sends us the import's details directly.
- The AI assistant answers questions about your numbers any time; see [Ask SaleTides AI](/ai/assistant).

## Feature requests

We'd love to hear what would help. Email us with what you're trying to do; it tells us more than a feature name.

## Enterprise

For more than 50,000 orders a month or 25 stores, [request a quote](https://saletides.com/request-quote).
