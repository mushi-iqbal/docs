## Support channels

### Email support

The best way to reach us is by email: **support@saletides.com**

Include in your email:
- Your store URL or account email
- A clear description of the issue
- Any error messages you see (screenshots are helpful)
- When the issue started

## Response times

We aim to respond to all support emails within **1 business day** (Monday–Friday).

## Enterprise support

Enterprise customers receive prioritized support with faster response times. Contact us at support@saletides.com to discuss Enterprise options.

## Reporting a bug

If you've found a bug in SaleTides, please email support@saletides.com with:
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Your browser and operating system
- Screenshots or screen recording if possible

We take all bug reports seriously and will acknowledge receipt of your report.

## Feature requests

We love hearing from customers about what would make SaleTides more useful. Send feature ideas to support@saletides.com with "Feature Request:" in the subject line.
