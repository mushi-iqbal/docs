---
title: "Subscriptions not showing"
description: "What to check when subscription reports are missing or empty."
---

## Prerequisites

Subscription reports need the **WooCommerce Subscriptions** extension on your store. Other subscription plugins store their data differently and aren't supported yet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the import">
    Subscriptions are imported with the rest of your store. If they're still importing, the import page or the banner at the top of the app says so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the date range">
    The Subscriptions list shows subscriptions **started** in your dates. Choose **All Time** to see every one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check filters">
    Clear any Smart Filter or saved filter on the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-import subscriptions">
    **Settings → Tools → Subscriptions → Reimport** fetches them again; it never creates duplicates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Still not working?

If the **Subscriptions** menu is missing altogether, or a re-import finds none, email [support@saletides.com](mailto:support@saletides.com) with your store address.
