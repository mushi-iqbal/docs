---
title: "Changing or cancelling your plan"
description: "Switch plans, move between monthly and yearly, or cancel, from Billing."
---

Open **Billing & Invoices** from the menu under your name (store owner only) and click **Manage subscription**. The secure Stripe billing portal opens.

## Changing plan

In the portal, choose **Update plan** and pick the new plan or billing period (monthly or yearly).

- Changes take effect straight away. The difference for the rest of your billing period is worked out to the day: an upgrade adds it to your next invoice, a downgrade credits it.
- Only plans that cover your number of stores are offered. To move to a smaller plan, remove stores first.

## When you go over your allowance

If your stores take more orders in the last 30 days than your plan includes, a notice in the dashboard names the plan that fits and links to Billing. Nothing is blocked; it's a prompt, not a limit.

## Cancelling

Choose **Cancel plan** in the portal. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, then your dashboard pauses and syncing stops. Your data is kept.

## Coming back

Subscribe again from the pricing page; syncing catches up on the orders placed while you were away.
