## What's included

Every report and feature for **30 days**, with one store. No credit card is needed to start.

During the trial SaleTides imports your **most recent 50,000 orders**. If your store has more, a notice says so, and your full history follows automatically when you subscribe. AI questions are limited to 15 a day.

## Trial status

A banner at the top of the app shows the days left, with **Upgrade Now**. We also email you 5 days and 2 days before the trial ends.

## Subscribing during the trial

Click **Upgrade Now**, choose a plan and billing period, and add your card. You're **not charged until the trial ends**: your first payment is taken on the day your trial would have ended, so you keep every free day.

## When the trial ends

If you haven't subscribed, your dashboard is paused and syncing stops. **Nothing is deleted.** Subscribe any time and everything picks up where it left off; syncing catches up on the orders placed in the meantime.
