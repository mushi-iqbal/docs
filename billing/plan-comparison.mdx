## How orders are counted

Orders are counted across all the stores on your account over the last 30 days, leaving out failed, trashed and draft orders. Renewals count, and refunded orders still count.

**Going over doesn't stop anything.** Your data keeps syncing and every report keeps working. If your stores go over your plan's allowance, the account owner sees a notice in the dashboard suggesting the plan that fits, and can switch from **Billing**.

## Which plan?

- **Solo**: one store doing up to about 15 orders a day.
- **Growth**: a growing store, or up to three stores.
- **Pro**: established stores and multi-store owners, up to about 330 orders a day.
- **Scale**: high-volume stores and agencies.
