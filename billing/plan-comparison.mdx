## Pricing overview

SaleTides uses a usage-based pricing model. **Every feature is included on every plan** — the only difference between plans is the number of stores you can connect and your monthly order volume.

| | Solo | Growth | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly price** | $9/mo | $49/mo | $99/mo |
| **Annual price** | $7/mo *(save 2 months)* | $41/mo *(save 2 months)* | $83/mo *(save 2 months)* |
| **Stores** | 1 store | Up to 10 stores | Unlimited stores |
| **Orders/month** | 5,000 | 25,000 | 100,000 |
| **All features** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Free trial** | 30 days | 30 days | 30 days |

## Choosing the right plan

**Solo ($9/mo)** is right if you:
- Run a single WooCommerce store
- Process fewer than 5,000 orders per month

**Growth ($49/mo)** is right if you:
- Manage multiple WooCommerce stores (up to 10)
- Process up to 25,000 orders per month

**Pro ($99/mo)** is right if you:
- Have more than 10 stores, or an unlimited number
- Process up to 100,000 orders per month
- Run a high-volume or agency-scale operation

## Enterprise

For stores exceeding 100,000 orders/month, or for custom requirements (SLAs, dedicated support, custom integrations), contact us for an **Enterprise** quote at [saletides.com/enterprise](https://saletides.com/enterprise).

## Annual billing

Choose annual billing to get **2 months free** (equivalent to ~17% savings). Annual plans are billed upfront for the full year.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Annual savings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo | $9 | $7 | $24/year |
| Growth | $49 | $41 | $96/year |
| Pro | $99 | $83 | $192/year |
