## Payment

SaleTides takes payment by card through Stripe. Your card details are held by Stripe and never touch our servers.

## Invoices

**Billing & Invoices** (menu under your name) shows your current plan, next billing date, payment method, billing email and invoice history. **View →** opens an invoice, where you can download it as a PDF.

## Updating your card or billing details

Click **Manage subscription** to open the Stripe portal, then:

- **Payment method**: add a new card or change the default.
- **Billing information**: your company name, address and tax ID, which appear on future invoices.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we email you and Stripe retries the card over the following days. You keep full access while it retries. If every retry fails, the subscription is cancelled and your dashboard pauses until you subscribe again; your data is kept.
