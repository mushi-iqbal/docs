SaleTides connects to your WooCommerce store and turns its orders, customers, products and subscriptions into reports you can act on. It keeps itself up to date: new and changed orders arrive within seconds, and nothing needs exporting or uploading.

## New to SaleTides? Start here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your store">
    Enter your store's address and approve SaleTides in WooCommerce. It takes about two minutes. [Connecting your store →](/getting-started/connecting-your-store)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your dashboard opens with your recent orders">
    Your last 30 days of orders are imported first, so the dashboard opens in minutes. Older history fills in behind it. [Your first import →](/getting-started/understanding-your-first-import)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your way around">
    Every report shares the same date picker, comparison, filters and columns. [Finding your way around →](/getting-started/navigating-the-dashboard)
  </Step>
</Steps>
