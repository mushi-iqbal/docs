<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your WooCommerce store">
    Takes 2 minutes. We use WooCommerce's official OAuth — no API keys to copy and paste.

    [Connect your store →](/getting-started/connecting-your-store)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the import to complete">
    SaleTides imports your full order history in the background. Most stores are ready in under 20 minutes.

    [What gets imported →](/getting-started/understanding-your-first-import)
