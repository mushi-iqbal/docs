## Overview

The **Data Management** section in Settings gives you control over your store's data within SaleTides. Navigate to **Settings → Data Management**.

## Clear store data

**Clear all store data** removes all imported WooCommerce data from SaleTides for your current store — orders, customers, products, coupons, refunds, subscriptions, and all calculated metrics.

This does **not**:
- Delete your SaleTides account
- Affect your WooCommerce store
- Remove your store connection or API credentials

After clearing data, you can trigger a fresh import from your WooCommerce store to re-populate everything.

### When to use this

- Your WooCommerce store was restored from a backup and data is out of sync
- You want to start fresh after a major store migration
- You're testing SaleTides with dummy data and want to clear it before going live

<Warning>
Clearing store data is irreversible. All historical analytics, calculated metrics, and segment history will be removed. The only way to get the data back is to run a full re-import from WooCommerce.
</Warning>

## Delete account

**Delete Account** permanently removes your entire SaleTides account, including:
- All store data and analytics
- All saved segments
- All dashboard configurations
- All user accounts
- Billing and subscription records

Your WooCommerce store is not affected — only the SaleTides account and its data are removed.

<Warning>
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Before deleting, consider downloading any reports or exports you want to keep.
</Warning>

To delete your account, click **Delete Account** and confirm by typing your store name. You will receive a final confirmation email.

## Data export before deletion

Before clearing data or deleting your account, we recommend exporting any reports you want to retain:
- Orders export (CSV or Excel)
- Customer export
- Product export
- Any segment reports

See the individual analytics pages for export instructions.
