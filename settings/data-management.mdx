---
title: "Data management"
description: "Clear your store's imported data or delete your SaleTides account."
---

Both actions are in **Settings → Danger Zone**, only for the store owner, and can't be undone.

## Clear all store data

Deletes everything imported from your store (orders, customers, products, subscriptions and the rest) but keeps your store connection and account. Use it to start fresh, then re-import from **Settings → Tools**. While an import is running, clearing is blocked so nothing is half-written.

## Delete account

Deletes your SaleTides account, all its data and your store connection. SaleTides also removes its webhooks from your store, and if this was the last store on your subscription, billing stops.

<Warning>Export anything you want to keep first: [Exporting](/analytics/orders/exporting).</Warning>
