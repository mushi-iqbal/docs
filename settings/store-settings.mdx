Open **Settings** from the menu under your name. Only the store owner can change settings.

## Store connection

Shows your store, when SaleTides last checked with it, your latest order and whether your order history is complete. New and updated orders arrive within seconds through webhooks, and SaleTides double-checks your store every 15 minutes.

If syncing is paused (for example after a trial ends without a plan), it says so and picks up where it left off once your plan is active again.

### Reconnect store

If SaleTides can't reach your store (the card says **Sync behind**), or you deleted or regenerated its API key in WooCommerce, click **Reconnect store**. You're taken to WooCommerce's approval screen for the same store; click **Approve**. SaleTides saves the new key, switches its webhooks back on and catches up on orders that changed meanwhile. Your data and settings stay as they are, and nothing is imported again from scratch.

## Store settings

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Timezone | Which day an order falls on in every report. Set from your WooCommerce settings when you connect |
| Date format | How dates are shown |
| Week starts on | Weekly charts and the This Week / Last Week ranges |
| Currency | The currency symbol and format |
| Dashboard language | The language of SaleTides for everyone on this store |

## Sales reporting

Choose which **order statuses count as sales**. Completed and Processing are the default; some stores add On hold, or a custom status from a plugin. Every sales figure (gross and net sales, sales orders, product and category sales, customer lifetime value) uses this choice, and each status shows how many orders it holds.

## Notifications

- **Stock Alert Emails**: an email when products go out of stock or reach your low stock level, checked every hour.
- **Low stock level**: products at or below this many units count as low stock, here and on the Stock page.
- **Alert email address**: leave blank to use your account email.
