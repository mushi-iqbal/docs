---
title: "Team members"
description: "Invite people to your store's dashboard and choose which areas each can see."
---

Open **Manage Users** from the menu under your name. Only the store owner can manage users.

## Adding a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add User">Enter their name, email and a password for them.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose what they can see">Tick only the areas they need, or leave every area unticked for full access.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">They sign in at your store's SaleTides address with that email and password.</Step>
</Steps>

## Areas

| Area | Includes |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | The dashboard and its widgets |
| Orders | Orders, order pages and order exports |
| Refunds | The refunds list |
| Subscriptions | Subscriptions and their pages |
| Customers | Customers and their profiles |
| Products | Products, categories, variations, stock and top sellers |
| Coupons | Coupons and their pages |
| Reports | The Reports menu |
| Cohorts | The cohort reports |

Only the owner can open Settings, Billing and Manage Users, switch or add stores, and connect AI assistants.

## Changing access

**Edit Permissions** changes a team member's areas. You can also change their password, **deactivate** them to block sign-in straight away (they're signed out on their next click), or delete them.
