## The menu

The menu on the left groups the reports:

| Menu | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your key numbers and widgets you choose |
| **Reports** | Sales: Revenue, Orders, Order Sources, Refunds, Discounts & Store Credit, Devices. Customers: Customer Groups, Customer Retention, Customer Sources. Subscriptions: Subscriptions, Subscription Plans, Lifecycle, Retention, Events, Active Subscriptions |
| **Orders** | Every order, with filters, saved filters and exports |
| **Refunds** | Every refund, by the date it was issued |
| **Subscriptions** | Every subscription (with WooCommerce Subscriptions) |
| **Customers** | Every customer and their profile |
| **Products** | Products (with a Variations tab), Categories, Stock and Top Selling Products |
| **Coupons** | Every coupon and what it did |
| **Cohorts** | Returning Customers, By Order Counts, Orders per Customer, Average Order Value, Lifetime Value, Orders Over Time, Sales Over Time and Subscription Cohorts |

The search bar at the top finds orders, customers and products by name, email or number; press <kbd>/</kbd> to jump to it. The store switcher next to it moves between your stores.

## Dates and comparisons

<Frame caption="The date picker: presets on the left, or pick any range">
  <img src="/images/date-range-picker.png" alt="The date picker open, with presets like Today, Last 30 Days and This Month next to a two-month calendar" />
</Frame>

Every report has a date picker with presets (Today, Yesterday, Last 7/30/90 Days, This Month, Last Month, Year to Date, Last Year, All Time) or a custom range. Dates follow your store's timezone.

Next to it, choose what to compare with:

- **vs previous period**: the period just before (last month for "This Month", yesterday at this time for "Today", the same number of days before for a custom range).
- **vs same period last year**: the same dates a year earlier.
- **No comparison**.

Cards then show the change and the earlier figure, for example **▲ 12.5% vs previous period · was $9,960**. Green means better and red worse; for refunds and failed orders a rise is red.

## Columns

<Frame caption="Customize Columns: tick the columns you want and drag to reorder">
  <img src="/images/customize-columns.png" alt="The Customize Columns panel on the Orders list, with numbered markers on the button, a column and Save" />
</Frame>

The Orders, Customers, Products and Subscriptions lists let you choose their columns: click **Customize Columns** (1), tick the ones you want and drag them into order (2), then **Save** (3). Your choice is kept for you; teammates keep their own. **Reset to Default** brings back the original layout.

## Smart Filters and saved filters

Lists have a **Smart Filter**: type what you want to filter by (status, country, product, category, coupon and 30+ more), pick a condition and a value, and click **Search & Filter**. Combine as many as you need. The cards above the list follow your filters.

Save a combination with **Save Filter** and reuse it from **Saved Filters**, which also shows each saved filter's totals side by side. See [Saved filters](/advanced/segments/overview).

## Exports

**Export** on a list sends a CSV of what you're looking at (your dates, search and filters) to your email, usually within a few minutes. Download links work for 48 hours. See [Exporting](/analytics/orders/exporting).
