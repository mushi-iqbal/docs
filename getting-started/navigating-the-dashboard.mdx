## Dashboard overview

After logging in, you land on your main **Dashboard**. This is your home base — a customizable summary of your store's performance with key metrics and charts.

## Sidebar navigation

The left sidebar gives you access to every section of SaleTides:

| Section | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your customizable overview with widgets and charts |
| **Orders** | Full order list, order details, and order reports |
| **Customers** | Customer directory, groups, retention, and acquisition |
| **Products** | Product performance, variations, and category reports |
| **Revenue** | Revenue breakdowns by country, payment method, and more |
| **Refunds** | All refunds with trends and metrics |
| **Coupons** | Coupon usage stats and segment reports |
| **Subscriptions** | Subscription list, metrics, and cohort analysis *(feature-gated)* |
| **Cohorts** | Retention cohorts, LTV, and order frequency analysis |
| **Segments** | Create and manage custom segments |
| **Abandoned Carts** | Browse and recover abandoned shopping carts |
| **Sources** | Traffic source attribution (referrers, UTM, landing pages) |
| **Settings** | Store settings, users, billing, and localization |

## The main dashboard

The dashboard is made up of **widgets** — individual cards you can add, remove, reorder, and configure.

Default widgets include:
- **Revenue over time** — line chart of gross revenue
- **Orders over time** — chart of order volume
- **New customers** — new vs. returning customer trend
- **Top products** — best-selling products by revenue
- **Order summary by status** — breakdown of order statuses
- **Orders by country** — geographic breakdown

### Customizing widgets

Click **Add Widget** (top right of the dashboard) to add new widgets. Each widget has a title and can be configured for specific date ranges or metrics.

To reorder widgets, drag them by their handle. To remove a widget, click the **×** icon on the widget card.

To restore the default layout, click **Reset Widgets**.

## Date range selector

Every analytics page includes a **date range picker** at the top right. You can select:

- Pre-built ranges: Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month, Last 90 days, This year, Last year
- **Custom range**: pick any start and end date

The selected date range applies to all charts and metrics on that page.

## Filters

Most analytics pages include a filter panel. Common filters include:

- **Order status** (completed, processing, refunded, etc.)
- **Payment method**
- **Country**
- **Product or category**
- **Customer segment** (custom segments you've created)

## Switching stores

If you have multiple WooCommerce stores connected, use the **store switcher** in the top navigation to switch between them. Each store has its own independent dataset and dashboard.

## User menu

Click your avatar in the top right to access:
- **Profile** — update your name and avatar
- **Settings** — store configuration and user management
- **Billing** — your current plan and payment details
- **Logout**
