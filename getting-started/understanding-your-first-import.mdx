## Recent orders first

When you connect a store, SaleTides imports your **last 30 days of orders first**. As soon as they're in, your dashboard opens, usually within a few minutes. Your older orders, customers, products and other data keep importing in the background.

While that happens, a banner at the top of the app says so:

> Your last 30 days are ready. Older orders are still importing (12,400 of 50,000), so reports further back fill in as they arrive.

Reports covering the last 30 days are complete straight away. Reports that reach further back, like last year's comparisons or cohorts, fill in as the older orders arrive. The banner disappears once everything is in.

When your dashboard opens you'll also get a welcome email with a link to it.

## How long it takes

How soon the dashboard opens depends on how many orders you took in the last 30 days:

| Orders in the last 30 days | Dashboard opens in about |
|---|---|
| A few hundred | 1–2 minutes |
| A few thousand | 3–5 minutes |
| 50,000 | 15–20 minutes |

Older history then imports at roughly 1,200 orders a minute. SaleTides fetches 100 orders at a time and paces its requests so your store stays responsive for shoppers; a slow store server makes everything take longer.

## What gets imported

| Data | Notes |
|---|---|
| Orders | With line items, notes, refunds and order attribution (source, UTM, device) |
| Customers | Registered customers; guest orders are grouped into one profile per email |
| Products | Products, variations and categories |
| Coupons | Settings and every use in your orders |
| Subscriptions | With WooCommerce Subscriptions: subscriptions and their renewal orders |

<Note>
  During the free trial, SaleTides imports your **most recent 50,000 orders**. When you subscribe, the rest of your history follows automatically.
</Note>

## Tracking progress

Until your recent orders are in, the import page shows each part's progress and an estimate of the time left. If something stops, it shows why and lets you retry; only the missing pages are fetched again.

## After the import

New and changed orders arrive within seconds through WooCommerce webhooks, and SaleTides checks your store every 15 minutes for anything missed. You don't need to import again. If you ever do, see [Manual import](/integrations/manual-import).
