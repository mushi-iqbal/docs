SaleTides is an analytics app for WooCommerce stores. It imports your store's orders, customers, products, coupons, refunds and subscriptions, keeps them in sync, and turns them into reports that answer everyday questions: what's selling, who's coming back, what you gave away in discounts, and how your subscriptions are doing.

## What it covers

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sales" icon="chart-line">Gross and net sales, refunds by the date they were issued, discounts and store credit, tax and shipping, and new against returning customers.</Card>
  <Card title="Orders" icon="receipt">Every order with 30+ filter fields, saved filters, custom columns and CSV exports.</Card>
  <Card title="Customers" icon="users">Profiles that group a guest's orders by email, lifetime value, groups, retention and acquisition sources.</Card>
  <Card title="Products" icon="box">Product and category sales, best sellers, variations, stock levels and stock alerts.</Card>
  <Card title="Subscriptions" icon="arrows-rotate">For WooCommerce Subscriptions: MRR, ARR, signups, renewals, churn, plans and cohorts.</Card>
  <Card title="Cohorts" icon="table-cells">Retention, lifetime value and order value by the month customers first ordered.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You approve SaleTides in WooCommerce">
    SaleTides asks your store for API access through WooCommerce's own approval screen. There are no keys to copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your history is imported">
    The last 30 days come first so your dashboard opens quickly; the rest of your history follows in the background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It stays in sync">
    New and changed orders arrive within seconds through WooCommerce webhooks, and SaleTides checks your store every 15 minutes for anything missed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the numbers are counted

- **Sales statuses.** Sales figures count the order statuses you choose in **Settings → Store Settings → Sales Reporting** (Completed and Processing by default).
- **Refunds count when they're issued.** An order refunded next month stays a sale this month, and the refund comes off next month's net sales. That way a refund never changes a month you've already reported.
- **Your timezone and week.** Dates follow your store's timezone, and weeks start on the day you choose.

Every card has an <Icon icon="circle-info" /> that explains its number; the [Metrics glossary](/metrics/glossary) lists them all.

## Who it's for

SaleTides is built for WooCommerce store owners and the people who run stores for them: from a single shop to agencies with many stores on one account.
