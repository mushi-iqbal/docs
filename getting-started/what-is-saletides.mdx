## Overview

SaleTides is a dedicated analytics platform built exclusively for WooCommerce stores. It connects directly to your WooCommerce site, imports your historical data, and gives you a real-time dashboard of everything happening in your store — from revenue trends and customer behavior to product performance and subscription metrics.

Unlike generic analytics tools, every report, chart, and metric in SaleTides is purpose-built for ecommerce. You don't need to configure anything — connect your store and your data is ready in minutes.

## Key capabilities

- **Custom Segments** — Filter any report by any combination of order, customer, or product attributes
- **Cohort Analysis** — Track customer retention, LTV, and order frequency over time by signup cohort
- **Traffic Sources** — See which referrers, landing pages, and UTM campaigns drive your best customers
- **Abandoned Carts** — View and recover abandoned shopping carts
- **Data Exports** — Export any dataset to CSV or Excel at any time
- **Multi-store** — Connect and switch between multiple WooCommerce stores from one account
- **Real-time sync** — Data updates automatically via WooCommerce webhooks

## How it works

```
Your WooCommerce Store
        ↓
  SaleTides connects via WooCommerce REST API
        ↓
  Historical data is imported (orders, customers, products, etc.)
        ↓
  Webhooks keep data up-to-date in real time
        ↓
  Your SaleTides dashboard — always current
```

## Who SaleTides is for

SaleTides is designed for WooCommerce store owners, marketing teams, and analysts who want:

- A dedicated analytics tool (not a generic dashboard you have to configure yourself)
- Cohort and retention analysis without spreadsheets
- Customer segmentation without an expensive CRM
- Subscription metrics without a separate tool
