## Before you begin

- WooCommerce installed on a site you can sign in to as an administrator.
- Your site reachable over the internet (a site behind a login wall or on your own computer can't be connected).
- Pretty permalinks turned on in **WordPress → Settings → Permalinks** (anything except "Plain"), so the WooCommerce REST API works.

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your details">
    At [saletides.com/signup](https://saletides.com/signup), enter your name, email and a password. Your 30-day free trial starts here; no credit card is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your store">
    Enter your store's root address (for example `https://mystore.com`) and choose your SaleTides address: letters, numbers and hyphens, up to 63 characters. It becomes your dashboard's URL, like `mystore.saletides.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve SaleTides in WooCommerce">
    You're taken to your store's WooCommerce approval screen. Sign in if asked and click **Approve**. SaleTides asks for read and write access: read to import your data, write so it can set up the webhooks that keep it in sync. It never changes your orders, products or customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your dashboard opens">
    The import starts straight away. You'll see its progress until your last 30 days of orders are in, then your dashboard opens. [What happens during the import →](/getting-started/understanding-your-first-import)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets imported

Orders (with their line items, notes and refunds), customers, products and variations, product categories, coupons, and, if you use WooCommerce Subscriptions, subscriptions with their renewals.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The approval page shows an error or a blank page">
    A security plugin or firewall may be blocking `/wc-auth/`. Allow it for SaleTides, check your permalinks aren't set to "Plain", and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I approved but came back to an error">
    Your store may be blocking requests from our servers. Ask your host to allow requests to `/wp-json/wc/v3/`, then try again or [contact support](/troubleshooting/contacting-support).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to connect another store">
    Add it from the store switcher in the top bar. See [Multi-store support](/integrations/multi-store-support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
