Five cohort reports follow what each cohort spends and orders over time. All group customers by the period of their first order in your dates; rows are cohorts and columns are periods since.

## Lifetime Value

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cohort-ltv-table.png" alt="Lifetime value cohort table: cumulative spend per customer rising month by month" />
</Frame>

**Cohorts → LifeTime Value** shows the **cumulative** spend per customer, after refunds, for each cohort: Month 1 is what they spent in the month they joined, Month 6 what they'd spent by their sixth month, and so on.

Use it to decide what a new customer is worth. If customers typically reach $250 by month 12, you can afford to spend a good share of that to acquire one, and you can tell which months' customers were worth more.

## Average Order Value

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cohort-aov-table.png" alt="Average order value cohort table" />
</Frame>

**Cohorts → Average Order Value** shows the average order value of each cohort's orders in each period after they joined. Rising values mean returning customers buy more per order.

## Orders per Customer

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cohort-orders-per-customer.png" alt="Orders per Customer cohort report: average orders per customer by month" />
</Frame>

**Cohorts → Order Per Customer** shows the average number of orders per customer in each period, so you can see whether customers order more or less often as time goes on.

## Orders Over Time and Sales Over Time

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cohort-sales-over-time.png" alt="Sales Over Time cohort report: sales by month for each cohort" />
</Frame>

**Cohorts → Orders Over Time** counts each cohort's orders per period; **Cohorts → Sales Over Time** shows its sales (revenue less refunds) per period. Together they show how much of your monthly sales come from customers acquired long ago against recent ones.

## Using value cohorts

- **Budget acquisition** with Lifetime Value at month 6 or 12, not first-order value.
- **Spot a weak cohort early**: if a cohort's Month 2 is well below others, look at what was different when it joined (a deep discount, a new channel).
- **Holiday cohorts** often spend a lot at first and return less; compare their month 6 value with other months before repeating the campaign.
