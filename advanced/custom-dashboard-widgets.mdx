## What are widgets?

Widgets are the individual metric cards and charts that make up your main dashboard. SaleTides gives you full control over which widgets appear, how they're arranged, and what they're titled.

Navigate to your **Dashboard** to manage widgets.

## Default widgets

When you first log in, your dashboard comes with a default set of widgets:

- Revenue over time
- Orders over time
- New vs. returning customers
- Top products by revenue
- Orders by status (summary)
- Orders by country (summary)

## Adding a widget

Click **Add Widget** in the top right of the dashboard. A panel will appear with available widget types. Select the widget you want to add and configure it:

- **Title** — custom label for this widget
- **Type** — the metric or chart type
- **Date range** — optional override (otherwise inherits the global date range)

Click **Save** to add the widget to your dashboard.

## Available widget types

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Revenue chart | Gross revenue trend over time |
| Orders chart | Order count trend over time |
| New customers | New customer acquisition over time |
| Returning customers | Repeat customer trend |
| Order summary by status | Pie or bar chart of order statuses |
| Orders by country | Geographic breakdown of orders |
| Top products | Best-selling products by revenue |
| Refund rate | Refund rate trend |
| AOV trend | Average order value over time |
| Total revenue | Single metric card |
| Total orders | Single metric card |
| Total customers | Single metric card |

## Editing a widget

Hover over any widget to reveal the **Edit** (pencil) icon. Click it to update the widget's title or settings, then save.

## Reordering widgets

Click and drag any widget by its handle (top edge) to reorder it on the dashboard. The new order is saved automatically.

## Deleting a widget

Hover over any widget to reveal the **Delete** (×) icon. Click it to remove the widget from your dashboard.

Deleted widgets can be re-added at any time via **Add Widget** — no data is lost.

## Resetting to defaults

Click **Reset Widgets** to restore the default dashboard layout. This removes any custom widgets and resets the order to the original defaults.

<Warning>
Resetting widgets removes all your custom additions and ordering. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Per-store dashboards

If you have multiple WooCommerce stores connected, each store has its own independent dashboard. Widgets are configured separately per store.
