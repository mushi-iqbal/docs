Your dashboard is **fully customizable with drag and drop**. Choose which widgets it shows, drag them into any order, rename them and set how much each list shows. Your layout is saved to your account, so it looks the same every time you sign in.

## Adding a widget

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dashboard-add-widget.png" alt="The Add New Widget window building an Orders by type and status widget" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Widget">At the top right of the dashboard.</Step>
  <Step title="Pick a widget">Widgets already on your dashboard are marked **(on dashboard)**.</Step>
  <Step title="Name it if you like, and click Add Widget">It appears straight away in its section: the page scrolls to it and it glows for a moment so you can spot it. No reload needed.</Step>
</Steps>

## Build your own: orders by type and status

**Orders by type & status** counts exactly the orders you want. Choose:

- **Show**: the number of orders, their value, or a list of them.
- **Order types**: regular orders, subscription sign-ups, renewals, resubscriptions and subscription switches.
- **Statuses**: any mix, for example Failed and On hold to watch payment problems, or Processing to see what's waiting to ship.

## Arranging your dashboard: drag and drop

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dashboard-customize.png" alt="Customize mode: a note explaining drag and drop above widgets with drag handles, edit and remove buttons" />
</Frame>

Click **Customize** to turn on Customize mode. A note at the top of your widgets reminds you how it works. Then:

- **Drag and drop** any widget by its handle (⠿, left of its title) to move it anywhere on the page. The new order is **saved automatically** as soon as you drop it.
- **Edit** a widget with the pencil to rename it or change how many rows a list shows.
- **Remove** a widget with the cross. You can add it back any time from **Add Widget**.
- **Reset layout** brings back the default dashboard. It only appears in Customize mode, because it replaces your arrangement.

Click **Done** when you're finished.
