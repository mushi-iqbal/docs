---
title: "Saved filters"
description: "Save any combination of Smart Filters, reuse it in a click, and compare saved filters side by side."
---

A **saved filter** (sometimes called a segment) is a set of Smart Filter conditions you keep, like "Orders from the Subscriptions category" or "Customers who spent over $500 and haven't ordered in 90 days".

## Where you can use them

Orders, Customers, Products, Variations, Subscriptions, Coupons and Refunds each have their own saved filters.

## What you can do

- **Load** one from **Load a Saved Filter** above the list. The list, cards and export follow it, so it's quick to check a group every week.
- **Compare** them on the **Saved Filters** tab, which lists each saved filter with its totals side by side: for orders, orders, gross sales, items, average order value and items per order.
- **Export** a saved filter's records, or the comparison table, to CSV.
- **Delete** one with the bin icon.

## How saved filters stay current

A saved filter keeps its **conditions**, not a list of records. Every time you load it, it finds whatever matches now, in the dates you've picked. A customer who spends past your threshold appears in "Big spenders" the next time you look.

Saved filters are shared by everyone on the store.
