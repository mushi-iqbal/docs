## Weekly check-ins

Load the same saved filter each Monday with **Last 7 Days** and **vs previous period**: the cards show how that group did against the week before, with the earlier figures.

## Compare groups

The **Saved Filters** tab lists every saved filter with its totals in one table. Save "Mobile orders", "Desktop orders" and "Tablet orders" (Customer Device) and compare their average order value at a glance; or "Coupon orders" against "Full-price orders".

## Export for email campaigns

Load a customer saved filter, like "Clothing buyers who haven't ordered in 90 days", and **Export**: the CSV lands in your inbox, ready to import into your email tool.

## Follow a cohort of customers

Customers' **First Order** and **Bought from Category** conditions let you follow, say, everyone who first bought in last year's holiday season and what they've bought since.
