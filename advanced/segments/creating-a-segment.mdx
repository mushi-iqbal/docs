---
title: "Creating a saved filter"
description: "Build a Smart Filter, check the results, and save it with a name."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">Orders, Customers, Products, Variations, Subscriptions, Coupons or Refunds.</Step>
  <Step title="Add your conditions">
    Type a field in the **Smart Filter** box (or click a **Quick add** chip), choose a condition and a value. Add as many rows as you need; every row must match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the results">Click **Search & Filter** and look at the list and cards.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Click **Save Filter**, give it a clear name like "UK repeat buyers" and save.</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/smart-filter.png" alt="A Smart Filter on the Orders list with Product Category includes Subscriptions, and the Save Filter button" />
</Frame>

To change a saved filter, load it, adjust the conditions, save it under a new name and delete the old one.

## Ideas to start with

| List | Saved filter | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Orders | Subscription orders | Product Category includes Subscriptions |
| Orders | Failed payments | Order Status is Failed |
| Orders | Big orders | Order Total greater than 150 |
| Orders | Campaign orders | UTM Campaign equals spring_sale |
| Customers | VIPs | Total Spent greater than 500 |
| Customers | Lapsed buyers | Order Count greater than 1, Last Order before a date three months ago |
| Customers | Clothing buyers | Bought from Category includes Clothing |
| Products | Low stock sellers | Stock Status is instock, Stock Quantity less than 5 |
| Subscriptions | Annual plans | Billing Period equals year |
