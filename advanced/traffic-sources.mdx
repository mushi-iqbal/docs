SaleTides reads WooCommerce's order attribution for each order: the referring site, the page the customer landed on, UTM tags and the device they used.

## Order Sources

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/order-sources.png" alt="Order Sources: referral sources with orders, revenue and average order value" />
</Frame>

**Reports → Order Sources** groups sales orders by:

| Tab | Groups by |
|---|---|
| Referrals | The referring site (Google, Facebook, direct and so on) |
| Landing | The first page the customer landed on |
| UTM | UTM source, medium and campaign |

Each row shows orders, revenue and average order value, compared with your comparison period.

## Devices

**Reports → Devices** shows orders by operating system and by browser, with their sales and average order value. To filter orders by device type (desktop, mobile, tablet), use the Customer Device Smart Filter on the Orders list.

## Attribution

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where does the data come from?">
    WooCommerce 8.5 and later record each order's source when the customer checks out (WooCommerce **Order Attribution**). SaleTides imports those fields with the order.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are some orders 'Unknown' or direct?">
    Orders placed before your store recorded attribution, orders created in the WordPress admin, and visits where the browser didn't pass a referrer have no source.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I filter orders by source?">
    Yes. On the Orders list, use the Smart Filter fields Referral Site, Referral Type, Landing Path, UTM Campaign / Medium / Source / Content / Term, Customer Device and Operating System.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For where your *customers* (rather than orders) came from, see [Customer sources](/analytics/customers/acquisition-sources).
