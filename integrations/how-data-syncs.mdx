---
title: "How data syncs"
description: "Webhooks deliver changes within seconds, a check every 15 minutes catches anything missed, and webhooks are repaired automatically."
---

SaleTides keeps your data current three ways.

## 1. The first import

When you connect, SaleTides imports your history through the WooCommerce REST API: your last 30 days of orders first, then everything else. See [Your first import](/getting-started/understanding-your-first-import).

## 2. Webhooks, within seconds

SaleTides registers WooCommerce webhooks on your store, so WooCommerce tells it the moment something changes:

| Data | Changes sent |
|---|---|
| Orders | Created, updated, deleted |
| Customers | Created, updated, deleted |
| Products | Created, updated, deleted |
| Coupons | Created, updated, deleted |
| Subscriptions | Created, updated, deleted |

A new order usually appears in SaleTides within seconds. A status change (Processing to Completed, or a refund) updates it the same way.

## 3. A check every 15 minutes

Webhooks can be lost: a store under load, a plugin that blocks outgoing requests, a server restart. So every 15 minutes SaleTides asks your store for orders changed since its last check and imports anything it hasn't seen. If a webhook was lost, the order still arrives within about 15 minutes.

Every hour it also checks its webhooks on your store. WooCommerce switches off a webhook after repeated delivery failures; SaleTides switches it back on.

## What isn't synced in real time

- **Product categories** and **refund details** come with the import and with each order or product change, not on their own.
- **Changes to your WooCommerce settings** (timezone, currency) are read when you connect. Change them in [SaleTides settings](/settings/store-settings) too.

## When syncing pauses

If your trial ends without a plan, or a subscription ends, syncing pauses and the webhooks are switched off. When your plan is active again they're switched back on and SaleTides catches up on everything that changed meanwhile.

**Settings → Store Connection** shows when SaleTides last heard from your store.
