The import page shows each part's progress and, if something stops, the reason.

| The import page says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Your store is blocking our requests (HTTP 403)** | Usually Cloudflare or a security plugin. Ask your host to allow requests from SaleTides to `/wp-json/wc/v3/`, then click **Retry** |
| **Your store is rate limiting our requests** | Click **Retry**. SaleTides backs off and tries again more slowly |
| **Your WooCommerce REST API key is invalid or doesn't have permission** | In **WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API**, check the SaleTides key exists with Read/Write access, then **Retry**. If it was deleted, [contact support](/troubleshooting/contacting-support) |
| **We couldn't connect to your WooCommerce store** | Check your site is online and its address hasn't changed, then **Retry** |
| **Import stopped. Click Retry to resume** | Click **Retry**: it resumes from where it stopped |

During the free trial, stores with more than 50,000 orders get their newest 50,000; a notice says so, and the rest follows when you subscribe.

## The import seems stuck

An import that hasn't moved for 15 minutes is marked stuck. SaleTides restarts stuck imports automatically every few minutes, fetching only what's missing. Very large stores on slow servers can take hours; if the count keeps rising, it's working.

## Data looks incomplete after the import

- Check your [sales statuses](/settings/store-settings#sales-reporting): orders in other statuses don't count as sales, though they're on the Orders list.
- On the free trial, only your newest 50,000 orders are imported.
- Compare the right dates: SaleTides uses your store's timezone.

See [Data discrepancies](/troubleshooting/data-discrepancies) for more.

## Getting help

**Report** on the import page sends us its details. Or email [support@saletides.com](mailto:support@saletides.com) with your store address.
