---
title: "Multiple stores"
description: "Run several WooCommerce stores from one account, each with its own dashboard, on one subscription."
---

## Adding a store

Open the store switcher at the top of the app and click **Add Another Store**, then connect it as you did the first. Each store gets its own SaleTides address and its own data.

| Plan | Stores |
|---|---|
| Free trial, Solo | 1 |
| Growth | 3 |
| Pro | 10 |
| Scale | 25 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

## Switching stores

Pick a store in the switcher. Each store has its own dashboard, reports, settings, saved filters and team members, so a Spanish store can have Spanish dates and currency while another uses English.

## Billing

One subscription covers every store on the account. The plan's monthly orders are counted across all its stores together.

## Team members

Team members are added per store: someone helping with one store doesn't see the others. The account owner can switch between all of them.

## Removing a store

Open that store's **Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account**. It removes that store and its data; your other stores and subscription stay. If it's the last store on the account, billing stops.

## Agencies

Agencies often use Pro or Scale to keep every client store under one login and one invoice, adding each client's team as members of their own store only.
